Transportation BI

NYC Taxi Operations

How much of a raw trip file can be trusted, and what operating patterns remain after explicit quality rules?

Question

Separate valid and excluded records before reporting operational KPIs.

Method

Official TLC Parquet → Power Query → star schema → DAX → Power BI.

Finding

3,251,098 valid trips from 3,475,204 January rows; 93.55% validity.

January 2025 scope only. The methodology page reconciles every valid and excluded record.

03

Operations analytics

U.S. Airline Delay Analytics

Where and when are domestic flight disruptions most common, and which patterns should operations teams investigate first?

Question

Compare airports, routes, carriers, and departure periods with fair volume thresholds.

Method

36 BTS files → Power Query and SQL → Python statistics → executive Excel model.

Finding

20,928,579 scheduled records; 77.5% weighted on-time rate across 2023–2025.

Associations are not causal findings; network, weather, seasonality, and route mix remain important context.
